THE VISION

Not just a story.
A space of possibilities.

ScenarioCore is an exploration of decision-driven audiovisual experiences: stories designed to respond to the paths people choose.

01 / STRUCTURE
⌘

Designed narratives

Structured scripts and defined decision points create a foundation for coherent, branching experiences.

02 / CHOICE
⑂

Meaningful decisions

Choices determine which narrative route unfolds next, with the potential for paths to diverge or reconnect.

03 / EXPERIENCE
▷

Generative visuals

AI-assisted video generation is being explored as a way to bring scripted moments to life.

OUR APPROACH

Creativity, with
structure beneath.

Our proposed architecture separates narrative logic from media generation. A deterministic layer defines the story graph and resolves decisions, while generative tools can render selected scenes.

This approach is intended to make experiences more coherent, controllable, and measurable as the platform develops.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT STATUS
The future is still being written.

ScenarioCore is in early development. An initial .NET proof of concept has successfully generated a short video using the Runway API. The broader interactive simulation engine and decision-driven experience are planned, not yet released.
